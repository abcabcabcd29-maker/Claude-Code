Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026 · Version 2

In one sentence

Your journal and reflections live only on your phone. We have no account system and no history on our servers. When generating a response, the relevant text is sent to an AI provider once, processed, and discarded — never stored, never used for training. We don't sell data or run ads.

What we collect

Content you actively write, speak, or photograph (a Drop note, Chat conversation, photo, mood reading) — these exist only in your phone's local storage (AsyncStorage). We have no server-side copy and no account system that could access them.

We also keep anonymous usage statistics: which features were opened (e.g. "opened About You"), app version number, and a randomly generated device ID not linked to your identity. This statistics data contains none of your written content — only event-level information.

Crash monitoring counts only two numbers — whether a crash occurred and whether it was fatal — without collecting error details or stack traces.

Does your content leave your device

Yes, but only in one case: when AI needs to generate a response, journal entry, or reflection for you. At that moment, the relevant text is sent to our AI provider once. Processing is immediate and one-shot — we never store it in our own database or use it to train any model, ours or third-party.

Our primary AI provider is Volcengine Ark (ByteDance's platform, running DeepSeek V4 Flash). For certain scenarios (visual understanding, fallback when quota is exhausted), we also call Zhipu GLM. Regardless of provider, the logic is the same: called only when you actively write, never for training.

If your content involves self-harm or crisis situations, it is never sent to any external AI service — it stays entirely on your device.

Third-party services

  • Volcengine Ark (ByteDance) — processes your text to generate responses, journal entries, and reflections. Currently runs DeepSeek V4 Flash. Called only when you actively write; never used for training.
  • Zhipu GLM — visual understanding (photo recognition) and fallback text processing when primary quota is exhausted. Also called only on your active trigger; never for training.
  • Relay server — your content passes through our proxy server before reaching the AI providers above. The relay does not store or log content. Its purpose is to manage API keys server-side and prevent key exposure in the app binary — a security measure on our end that does not change how your content is handled.
  • Anonymous analytics endpoint — receives only content-free event records (event name + random device ID + version + timestamp), as described in "What we collect" above.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party beyond those listed above, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

Export — the Data Sovereignty Center lets you export all your records at any time. Take them, back them up on your own terms — we don't interfere.

Delete — "Permanently delete all data" in the Data Sovereignty Center erases everything stored locally (records, journals, reflections, usage statistics, device ID). This is a true deletion, not a soft hide — no copy remains on this device. This action is irreversible; please export what you want to keep before confirming.

About AI-generated content

AI-generated content within the app (responses, draft journals, reflections, mirror ritual card readings, etc.) is clearly labeled and never presented as human-written or professional advice. The app does not provide psychological diagnosis or replace professional medical/mental health services.

Minors

This app is intended for users who can independently understand and agree to this policy. If you are a minor, please use it with the knowledge and consent of a guardian.

Changes to this policy

If this policy undergoes material changes (e.g. new data types collected, new third-party services added), we will update the version number and you will see the consent page again the next time you open the app — we will never retroactively apply new practices under old consent.

Contact us

Any questions or feedback — reach us via WeChat (see the feedback entry in the Data Sovereignty Center). We actually read every message.
